The road and scenery ground use Land Steiermark / GIS-Steiermark, the current
airborne-laser-scanning DGM mosaic, retrieved 2026-11-12. The service combines
older 1 m terrain tiles with newer 2022–2023 0.5 m tiles at this venue. The
retained numeric exports do not identify a single flight epoch for every pixel.

[Official source and reuse terms](https://www.landesentwicklung.steiermark.at/cms/ziel/143660187/DE/)
permit reuse under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit Land Steiermark / GIS-Steiermark. Modifications are bounded numeric
export, CRS transformation, bilinear sampling, a 2 m road-profile noise filter
and relative datum subtraction. Road and scenery use the same height origin.

The original F32 road and scenery GeoTIFFs, exact export requests and service
metadata are retained here. Rendered hillshade colours are not height samples.
