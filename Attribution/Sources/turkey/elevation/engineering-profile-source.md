The longitudinal road is an original numerical reconstruction of Figure 1.1
and the quantitative climb/drop descriptions in Ethem Ersöz, *Development of a
racing strategy for a solar car*, Middle East Technical University, 2006.
[Repository](https://open.metu.edu.tr/handle/11511/16582);
[source PDF](https://open.metu.edu.tr/bitstream/handle/11511/16582/index.pdf).

The PDF and extracted diagram remain ignored research references. No thesis text,
chart image or reference-video pixels are shipped. The retained numeric trace,
calibration observations, chainage registration and original reconstructed road
controls are documented in elevation-corrections.json. The Appendix A gradient
vector is retained as rejected evidence because it does not close the circuit.

The [current operator](https://tosfedistanbulpark.com/en/the-circuit/) independently
publishes the 8.145% maximum grade used to constrain the reconstruction. This
profile remains an estimate with 2 m vertical and 25–60 m station uncertainty;
an as-built road survey would take precedence.
