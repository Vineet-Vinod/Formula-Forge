# Finite mixed-natural ravine base

The government 2019 orthophoto and the retained 2012/2014 ravine views qualify
the open-ravine outline as mixed planted/rocky hillside rather than asphalt.
This general land use does not identify exact under-canopy soil, plant species,
or a continuous lawn or exposed-rock sheet.

The material pass uses the already qualified, clipped domain from
`ravine-source.json`/`ravine-outline.json`. Only its remaining Runoff faces
change material; the photographed rock/ivy bands, paths, buildings, belvedere
and held roof remain separate. The private replay selected6283faces covering
432.4474m² and retained all positions, normals, UVs, row order and counts exactly.
The original1–2m outline uncertainty remains; y344m is a grid crop, not a slope
toe. No new height, support, contact, planting pose or native field is inferred.

The new Monaco-only material mixes existing licensed Interlagos grass and Monza
mineral textures with low-frequency procedural variation. Tint, texture scale
and the mix are appearance estimates for fresh native comparison. No photograph
or game-reference pixels are used as a texture. Canopy ground-row guards run
before this material-only pass; their actual supporting vertex payloads remain
exact after it.

Reference receipts, original images and the source-only Luna review are retained
under `build/reviews/resume-20261114/monaco/` and its `reviews/` sibling. Primary
orthophoto registration and exclusions are versioned in `ravine-outline.json`.
