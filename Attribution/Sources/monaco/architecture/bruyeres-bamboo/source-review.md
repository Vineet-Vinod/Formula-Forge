## Source and shape assessment

The dated 28 September 2025 owner photograph and the delivered-project photograph support a dense, irregular narrow-leaf mass around and below the left rounded balconies. The canes are mostly hidden by overlapping sprays; the foliage arches and droops rather than forming evenly spaced conifer-like tiers. Separate palms rise above it. V3's fuller foliage represents that source better than the sparse exposed-cane version. In both reviewed camera studies, the foliage stays in the existing planted strip: it does not cover the upper floors, cross the portal reserve, or reach the circuit protection.

The retained 220 procedural roots, soil bed, retaining walls, palms, six understory clumps, building envelope, portal reserve, and unrelated City rows remain unchanged. The candidate replaces 5,280 cane rows with 390,084 foliage rows while preserving all 245,415 other City rows in order and the Street payload byte-for-byte. The proof reports no face collapses, no triangles outside the bed, and no house or portal intersections. The minimum canopy clearance to the protection skin is 10.368 m.

The height choice is plausible as a bounded reconstruction, not a measurement. The photo-relative method gives roughly 5–7 m for typical foliage and allows taller tips; the candidate uses 4.23–8.12 m stems with a 14.7–16.4 m absolute leaf envelope and ±2 m uncertainty. The dated photo's facade controls are manually selected; the building base is itself estimated ±1.5 m, and foreground depth, camera pitch, occlusion, season, and individual root locations are not surveyed. Those limits are carried honestly in the source plan. The aerial garden imagery supports a planted site but cannot independently fix individual cane positions or heights.

## Limits before acceptance

The technical images use approximate flat lighting, opaque foliage/fence cards, and omit native transparency, shadows, cockpit, and runtime materials. They establish placement and massing only. Before treating this as accepted scenery, inspect the cooked material/shadow response and a fresh driving view; check that leaf-card opacity does not become a solid wall and that the dense canopy does not alias or flicker. No species identification or botanical census is supported.

**Result:** The frozen V3 candidate is ready to advance to native material and cooked visual review within the same bounded garden footprint. No source geometry correction is requested from this review.
