# Upper garden and bank reconstruction

This finite candidate replaces the coarse visual terrain under the upper garden
with retained IGN RGE height samples. The existing native field uses a roughly
12.05 m grid. Coincident-point checks against the retained upper grid show
metre-scale interpolation differences, including 6.28 m at one exposed point.
The 2 m authoring query spacing is not source resolution or accuracy. IGN states
that accuracy varies by measurement source, and the source epoch is unverified.

The government 2019 orthophoto locates roofs, garden, retaining edges and paths.
The 2012 ravine and 2014/2024 chapel oblique photographs establish the mixed
vegetation, exposed pale rock and built retaining context. Ground beneath canopy
is obscured. They do not measure a new profile or precise material boundaries.
The bounded form trial received Luna High source review on 15 November 2026;
cooked visual approval remains required.

The explicit 368.3183 m² domain excludes OSM and IGN roofs by 2.5 m, pedestrian
paths and racing protection by 2 m, and the previously qualified ravine. A 4 m
smooth collar ties back to the exact original outer planes. Render cells are
0.5 m. The section near northing 376 m, easting -59 to -55 m has a local rise
and fall from the constrained join and must be inspected in the cooked frame.
The packed private replay has no unresolved inward normals; it is not visual
acceptance. Roads, contact, native terrain, buildings and public paths stay fixed.

The only mapped tree rooted in this patch is OSM node 5931906971. Its existing
102 triangles must reproduce exactly before replacement. Its XY, seed, estimated
11 m height and whole shape stay fixed. Its vertical placement follows the new
actual packed ground at the four trunk corners with 0.10 m minimum burial.
Other tree rows remain exact. This support adjustment is separately source
reviewed, and does not establish a surveyed tree height or ground datum.

Existing photographed rock polygons receive a world-aligned licensed dry-rock
texture. Qualified mixed-natural ground receives the same rock with slope and
procedural grass variation. The map is an appearance proxy, not Monaco
photogrammetry or a geological identification. Roofs, aprons and paths remain
separate. The retained source receipts are in
`../textures/rock-boulder-dry/source.json`. Tiling, normal seams, blend appearance
and mask boundaries require a cooked comparison before acceptance.
