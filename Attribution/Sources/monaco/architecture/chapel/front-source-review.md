The 2019 photograph is strong evidence for broad front stairs and a paved forecourt: it shows multiple wide risers and a nearly level landing continuing across the main entrance frontage. The proposed 18.27 m × 8 m finite domain, 6.97 m apron datum, and five risers totaling 0.88 m are plausible bounded reconstructions. The apron datum is tied to the central coincident RGE query; the flank samples differ by up to 0.77 m. Those values justify a private test but do not measure the actual terrace plane or threshold. Keep the stated approximately ±1 m authoring uncertainty and the RGE variable-accuracy caveat.

The church and bridge plans are separate by 18.27 m at the front centre, with no plan overlap; a false bridge-clearance collision is not a reason to move either feature. The proposed exclusions for trees, other buildings, racing, and protection are appropriate for this finite study.
