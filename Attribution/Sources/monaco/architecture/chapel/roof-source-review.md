## Tower registration

The church photos show a separate, narrow bell tower to the viewer-right of the front. The ortho’s pale crown at pixel `(335,459)` falls at source `(-23.462, 320.834)` and agrees with that tower’s location beside the church’s right/front corner. A roughly square shaft around `2.3 × 2.3 m`, with about ±0.5 m size and ±1 m placement uncertainty, is a reasonable private reconstruction envelope. The open arched bell stage and small pale cupola/crown match the photos. Keep the proposed crown near 24 m absolute with ±3 m uncertainty as an architectural estimate; the ortho locates the crown plan, not its height. Exact foundation contact, hidden lower shaft, and the shaft-to-roof join still need packed-ground and geometry checks.

## Nave roof

The ortho and side view support a terracotta pitched roof over the mapped church, distinct from the decorative front screen. The mapped footprint is a sound plan boundary for a bounded roof study. The proposed eaves range of 13.5–14.6 m absolute is compatible with the retained IGN boundary observations, with the stated source uncertainty. Do not use the 15.2–15.9 m IGN vertices at the rear as nave-ridge heights: those vertices extend into the neighboring flat-roof annex.

The bright longitudinal strip in the ortho runs near `u=9.5–10 m`, while the front gable center is around `u=7.13 m`. The photos and aerial do not establish whether that strip is the nave ridge, an aisle/roof junction, or flashing. Keep its role and exact roof partition held; do not force it to the gable center. A broad `17.5 ± 2.5 m` ridge estimate (eaves plus an estimated roof rise) is reasonable for a private silhouette study, provided the ridge line and pitch remain explicitly estimated and are not described as measured.

Keep the extra grey annex outside the church footprint separate. This review supports a bounded architectural reconstruction only; it does not validate its ground contact, exact roof pitch, or appearance from any particular driving camera.
