# Villa Hermosa formal garden

The registered formal lawn was missing above a coarse sloping ground mesh. This bounded reconstruction adds a source-controlled lawn, pale balustrade and clipped hedge, and an original irregular-stone upper retaining face. Separate natural rock closes to the actual old ground below the stone band. The six-metre maximum stone height is a photo estimate with ±2.5 m uncertainty.

Both conflicting pool/apron outlines remain temporary source holds. They are not two claimed pools. The mapped tree retains its position, height, form and seed; only its root moves to the new garden, after its exact old family is found. All original ground rows remain unchanged beneath the new local garden. Source and camera approval permit a cooked trial; native appearance and performance remain open.
