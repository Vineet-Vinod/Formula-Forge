- OpenStreetMap footprint/tags remain covered by repository OSM attribution, ©OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright .
- National Library publication links are provenance references. No publication pages or long text excerpts are copied into assets.

Procedural geometry is authored from the source-visible architectural forms. These receipts do not imply survey accuracy or expand the repository's existing asset licence terms.
