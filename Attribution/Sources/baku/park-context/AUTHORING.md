# Rebuilding the authored public-ground mesh

Run `build/tmp/lasvegas-python/bin/python tools/rebuild_baku_park.py --check` to prove the authored source compiles to the versioned Ground patch. Omit `--check` to repack the identical reviewed output. The authoring schema and compiler are strongly typed; the real-asset regression also verifies byte-for-byte reconstruction.

`authored-ground.json.gz` contains 38,320 deduplicated vertices with source-local XYZ in metres, source-space normals and actual UVs. Its 55,257 indexed faces retain their material and tile names. It also contains exact original semantic removal witnesses, protected input hashes, source constraints and the reviewed output checksum. The compiler reflects coordinates, packs centimetres and builds the atomic GroundPatchSource. It needs no ignored scene parents, photos, PNG exports, SciPy wheels, network data or game process.

These controls record the approved site reconstruction. They are not a bare-earth survey. Source preparation used the original emitted planes, an estimated public field at Road + 0.15 m with ±2 m absolute uncertainty, 40 m public and 15 m retained-property collars, and exact outer ties. The office has a separate relative base adjustment. The archive also records its occupied-interior render cut, six literal mapped beds and a photographed verge with ±0.5 m width uncertainty. The compiler retains the authored coordinates, normals and UVs when road or terrain inputs change. Runtime source hashes and exact original-row guards reject a stale underlying scene.

To change the model, requalify source/public/property relations and the actual new+kept planes, author updated controls, rerun geometry/contact/scope checks, and obtain fresh matched native-camera review. A checksum change is a review boundary, not evidence of quality. Retain literal outside attributes, physical/native road and protection data, current unrelated City families and supported tree feet. Do not derive a new ground survey from the inherited GLO-90 DSM, flatten a whole park or move a cartographic category boundary as if it were an observed retaining edge.

The 98-tree recipe remains procedural in baku_park_trees.py and the retained canopy helpers. The 6,492-row office is an exact source-family Z translation. This control archive covers the ground, material roles and occupied-interior render geometry. Primary receipts and proofs bind the reference-only images and rejected fitting history by hash. This compile path needs no image pixels and copies none to game assets.
