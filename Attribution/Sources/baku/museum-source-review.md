# Baku Niyazi museum site identity and frontage

## Decision

**Source identity: NW C-shaped footprint = former Mariinski gymnasium; SE rectangular footprint = De Bour mansion.** The gymnasium’s principal Niyazi frontage is the three-segment stepped edge on OSM way 152110152 (edges 3/5/7), approximately 42.17 m in total. De Bour’s main entrance is the NE short end of relation 3977156 / outer way 211461711 (edge 1); its NW long side (edge 0) adjoins the glass connector. This is source qualification only, not runtime acceptance.

## Evidence

- The museum director’s first-person history identifies the first property as De Bour and the second as the former school/gymnasium, located higher up Niyazi Street. The mapped NW C footprint is the higher uphill parcel; the SE rectangle is downhill. This agrees with the observed route order.
- The supplied driving frames `niyazi-ref-06.jpg` (HUD 1:21.857) and `niyazi-ref-08.jpg` (1:22.857) show the articulated 4/3/4 gymnasium facade first along the right side after T15, with the domed De Bour building still farther ahead/downhill at the connector. These are consecutive same-lap views, not unrelated angle matches. SHA-256: `5b3a9b138224caed05d8eaf0197a54b2ae03304120035e031afc9ff21035df00` and `f805ddcde0576680a6e8a98b3c5438dd9e7cf5bba88386bfac0ca4c7aa62b067`.
- The official museum primary photos distinguish the two designs: `niyazi-official-02.jpg` shows the domed, long classical De Bour facade (SHA-256 `2b75e652f9074dba8ad71b76763065b64f2f3a1ac9c7585d020db537a1aba53c`); `niyazi-official-04.jpg` shows the glass connector and the separate rectilinear-window elevation (SHA-256 `7fdd7008387595dee9778b61ed5f3084264ee694a5c4143fb5810f6c15a16ae8`). Their perspective does not alone prove which exact De Bour OSM edge carries the principal entrance.
- The georeferenced footprint overlay `niyazi-esri-footprint-registration.png` (SHA-256 `64ed768aec4b0756ce08c7df044f429ba0b9b6a0c4b8159a214c690b2b05e9ec`) shows the C-shaped parcel’s Niyazi side as edges 3/5/7, lengths about 12.02/17.29/12.85 m. The 17.29 m center section is about 41% of the 42.17 m frontage, closely matching the primary-photo estimate of a wider central pavilion (about 39%) between four-bay wings. This is strong shape registration for the gymnasium, not a measured architectural plan.
- The SE rectangle’s 31.83 m short edge and 47.50 m long edge are both possible facade candidates from footprint alone. The dome and route order establish the De Bour identity there, but the available official photographs do not uniquely register its main doorway/front edge. Keep that detail provisional rather than transferring the gymnasium’s rhythm to it.
- De Bour’s main-end registration is resolved by combining the official primary image `niyazi-official-02.jpg` with the literal footprint and floor plans. In the photo the glass connector is on the image-right of the domed entrance. With a camera on Niyazi looking toward the NE short end, the connector on the rectangle’s NW long edge projects to image-right; viewing the opposite SW end would put it to image-left. The plans place the paired porticoes at a short end. The 2015/2014 full-frame De Bour photos corroborate the facade divisions: `de-bour-additional-preview-1.jpg` shows seven bays on the entrance, with arches at bays 0/3/6 and paired porticoes at 1/5; `de-bour-additional-preview-2.jpg` shows the distinct long SE side, with nine rectangular upper windows followed by a three-arch projecting gallery and ornate terminal bay. This qualifies edge 1 as entrance and edge 0 as connector-facing, while exact threshold/forecourt elevation remains held.
- The current De Bour private layout’s body cornice estimate of 14.4 ± 3 m and mansard rise of 4 ± 1.5 m are plausible broad architectural estimates for the photographed two principal levels plus crown. They are not surveyed dimensions; keep the dome/spire/crown silhouette distinct and do not turn them into a fixed absolute roof level.
- For the gymnasium, the unwarped full-sensor photo02 study estimates a 14.10–16.58 m band between the main upper cornice and lower balcony/two-upper-row band. The pinhole fit still has about 21 px maximum corner error and non-orthogonal raw axis cosine 0.105, so this is not a calibrated survey. A separate broad reconstruction schedule of central cornice 22 ± 4 m and wing cornice 20 ± 4 m is reasonable as an explicitly estimated architectural envelope. Photo03 supports facade rhythm, but its embedded Adobe perspective/lens correction metadata rules it out for metric scaling.
- Gymnasium door steps and threshold remain held. Its entrance-support diagnosis identifies a 6.08 m² door-front region on one coarse DSM-blend terrain plane; the 24.726 m candidate threshold is not qualified. Preserve current ground rather than flattening it or authoring those steps from this estimate alone.

## Limits

The earlier assignment of De Bour to the NW C and gymnasium to the SE rectangle should be retired. The old 18.5 m gymnasium estimate used the wrong frontage width; its mechanical rescaling to 24.5 m is not source evidence. The current 22 ± 4 m central / 20 ± 4 m wings schedule is instead a bounded architectural estimate informed by the unwarped photo02 band fit and visible low entrance row/parapet. Floor heights and facade perspective are not fully calibrated. Keep both buildings’ absolute base and roof elevations held pending stronger registration.
